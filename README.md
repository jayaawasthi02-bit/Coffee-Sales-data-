# Coffee Shop Sales & Product Analysis | SQL Server

## Project Overview

Analysed coffee shop sales and market data using Microsoft SQL Server to identify product performance, customer spending patterns, monthly sales trends, and city-wise revenue contributions.

## Business Problems Solved

Calculated sales count for each product.

Identified the top 3 selling products in each city.

Analysed city population and coffee consumers.

Calculated average sales and rent per customer.

Evaluated monthly sales growth.

Assessed market potential and city revenue contribution.

## Tools & Skills

Database: Microsoft SQL Server

SQL Concepts: Joins, CTEs, Subqueries, Aggregate Functions, Window Functions, DENSE_RANK(), Date Functions and Percentage Calculations.

## Key Insights

Identified top-performing products across different cities.

Evaluated city-wise sales performance and revenue contribution.

Analysed monthly sales growth to understand business trends.

Compared customer spending and market potential across cities.

## Business Recommendations

Prioritise high-demand products to optimise inventory.

Introduce targeted promotions to improve customer spending.

Focus on cities with higher market potential for expansion.

Monitor monthly sales trends to identify growth opportunities.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates the use of SQL Server to analyse retail sales data, solve business problems, and generate insights that support data-driven business decisions.
